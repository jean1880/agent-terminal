  - Built-in, per file: a "View diff" toggle on every file-edit card, showing a unified diff against
    the pre-turn checkpoint, in GtkSourceView with diff highlighting. The `Ctrl+Shift+D` repo panel

- 2026-10-06: **agy `--mode plan` is NOT read-only headless** (verified with a control, in both
  `-p` and stream-json). agy writes its plan to `~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/<id>/`, a
  `system_message` follows, and then it applies the edits itself (`replace_file_content`) while
  its reply says it only planned. Shell commands are auto-denied in every mode without
  `--dangerously-skip-permissions`; edits are not gated in any mode. So the no-hook "forced plan"
  fallback was false safety. Only the approval hook gives agy a real ask-before-edit. Evidence:
  scratchpad `agy-stream-plan.ndjson`, `agy-mode-*.json`.
